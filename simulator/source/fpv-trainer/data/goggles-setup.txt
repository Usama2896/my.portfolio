AL JEHAD FPV — GOGGLES / EXTERNAL VIDEO

1. First test on your normal monitor. Keep a keyboard available.
2. Check the exact Skyzone Cobra model and its manual for VIDEO INPUT.
   A USB socket may be power or firmware only; it does not necessarily accept PC video.
3. If your goggles have AV IN, a PC normally needs an ACTIVE HDMI-to-composite
   AV converter (HDMI INPUT, composite OUTPUT), power for that converter, and
   the correct Skyzone-compatible AV cable. A passive HDMI cable will not do.
   Check PAL/NTSC support, pinout and voltage in both device manuals before buying.
4. Connect the PC video output to the converter, then its composite output to
   goggles AV IN. Select AV input on the goggles. Follow their manual.
   If your exact model supports HDMI IN, use its documented HDMI input instead.
5. On Windows press Win+P and choose Duplicate. Start the simulator, open
   Drone & Controls and enable Goggles display mode. It makes the app fullscreen;
   it does not detect, pair with or wirelessly transmit to goggles.
6. V toggles the moving attitude reference. F11 exits fullscreen. Esc opens menu.
7. Analogue video reduces text clarity and may add delay. Test comfortably on
   the ground first. This is the same single FPV camera image, not stereo VR.

USB RadioMaster input and goggles video are separate connections. No radio
vibration is guaranteed: most transmitter USB joystick modes do not provide
rumble. The optional vibration setting only requests supported gamepad rumble.

Exact Cobra video hardware has not been physically tested in the cloud.
